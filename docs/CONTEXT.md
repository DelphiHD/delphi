# CONTEXT.md

Factual ground truth for HD Reports. Who Kaycee is, who the customers are, what the product is and is not, what's been decided.

This file gets injected into prompts that need to reason about the project at all. Keep it dense, current, and free of speculation. Strategic *intent* (where we are going) lives in `INTENT.md`. *Brand voice* lives in `IDENTITY.md` and `VOICE.md`. *Why-we-chose-X* lives in `DECISIONS.md`.

To be filled in during Phase 1 or early Phase 2: Kaycee's professional background and Human Design lineage; the customer profile (who is buying readings and why); the product surface (which reports, which pricing model, which delivery formats); the canonical glossary of HD terms we use consistently across reports.
