# IDENTITY.md

Brand and lineage voice for HD Reports. This file is injected into every report-generation prompt alongside `VOICE.md`. Together they teach the model to sound like Kaycee, not like a generic AI.

IDENTITY.md is the *lineage* layer: what tradition Kaycee writes inside (Ra Uru Hu's Human Design system, the Rave I'Ching, the BG5 / OC16 lineages where relevant); what she emphasizes and what she refuses to do; how she relates Human Design to adjacent systems (astrology, gene keys, etc.) without conflating them; what archetypes and metaphors are native to her writing and what would feel borrowed.

VOICE.md is the *mechanical* layer: sentence length, rhythm, vocabulary, em-dash policy (we do not use them), how she opens and closes a passage, how she handles second-person address.

To be filled in during Phase 3, before any report generation runs: lineage scope, language conventions, no-go words and frames, how to introduce technical HD vocabulary to readers new to the system.
