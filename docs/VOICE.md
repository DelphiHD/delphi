# VOICE.md

How Kaycee writes. Injected into every report-generation prompt alongside `IDENTITY.md`. IDENTITY.md is what tradition she writes inside; VOICE.md is the mechanical surface of her prose.

Rules that are already non-negotiable: no em dashes anywhere, ever. Use commas, periods, or colons. The lint catches violations; prompt-only enforcement leaks.

To be filled in during Phase 3, before any report generation runs: typical sentence length and rhythm; first-person versus second-person address; how she opens a passage; how she closes a passage; vocabulary preferences and banned phrases; how she handles uncertainty and qualification; tone calibration (warm but not sentimental, precise but not clinical).

Source material for this file: Kaycee's existing written readings, voice memo transcriptions, and any published writing. Phase 3 ingests these into the source library; this file distills the patterns that recur.
