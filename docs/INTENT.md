# INTENT.md

Strategic ground truth for HD Reports. What we are building, what we are deliberately not building, what success looks like.

This file gets injected into prompts that need to make scope or priority calls. CONTEXT.md is the facts; INTENT.md is the direction. When the two diverge, the work is to update one of them, not paper over the gap.

To be filled in during Phase 1: the one-paragraph product thesis; explicit non-goals (what this is not — for example, not a generic astrology tool, not a coaching platform, not a community); the launch bar (what has to be true on day one) and the post-launch bar (what we will measure to know it's working); long-term vision and the next two strategic decisions we expect to face.
